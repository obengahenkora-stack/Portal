What is the Students Dashboard?

The Students Dashboard brings everything tied to your index number into one place — your assessment scores across all courses, your attendance record and percentage, and any issue reports you've submitted along with the lecturer's replies. Instead of checking the Assessment Portal, Attendance, and Report Issue sections separately, you can see it all here at a glance. Sign in below (creating an account takes a minute) and link it to your index number once — after that, your dashboard loads automatically every time you sign in.
